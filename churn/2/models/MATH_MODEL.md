# /2 · Math model — Structures of number

The **math model** reads mathematical constants and canonical integer sequences
as *Structures*: standing quantities that are **definitional** — true by
construction, not measured, and carrying no uncertainty at all.

## The distinction the model keeps

Every math structure is **EXACT** — a definition. Unlike a physical constant
(which may be measured) or a social figure (which is observed over a period), a
mathematical constant has no uncertainty and no date: it simply *is*. The digits
shown are truncated decimal expansions of an exact object. This is the cleanest
kind of Structure — a standing quantity with no provenance beyond its own
definition.

## Provenance

Standard mathematical definitions (public domain); sequence identities
cross-referenced to the OEIS (On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences) by
A-number. These are not "sourced from the internet" in the sense of measured
data — they are mathematical facts, cited to their canonical reference.
