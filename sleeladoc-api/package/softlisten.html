SLSoftOffer #sleela 3.0 (lib/softlisten/SLSoftOffer.sleela)

SLSoftOffer class

Defines SLSoftOffer — a contribution someone has OFFERED to the standing system but that the consuming model has not agreed to yet. An offer is plain data: who offered it, at what trust, what it would do (a document edit, a module, a vertical-integration tier), the tokens it carries, the ties it claims to other things, and the findings the soft listeners have attached. It changes nothing until it is agreed.

softlisten-demo #sleela 3.0 (lib/softlisten/softlisten-demo.sleela)

SoftListenDemo class

Runnable, self-checking demonstrator for soft offers and soft listeners. Offers wait at a desk, unagreed, while the Malice, Ties and Loader listeners ascertain them: deny-listed lines and tokens, false and dead and undeclared ties, loader issues from a dry run of the hot-link linker, and conflicts with the live document. Shows HOLD findings that clear when the world changes, BLOCK findings that never do, the consuming model agreeing through the observer and the linker, and the soft listeners themselves being paused, altered and retired (failing closed). Prints PASS or FAIL per check and a tally.

Carries the standard Metadata Class record — eleven catalogued provenance attributes (version, id, author, date, copyright, trademark, corporation, origin, birth date, science rating, national schedule) with matching get/set accessors. These are omitted from the tables below for brevity.
