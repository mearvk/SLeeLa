## Three habits the system teaches

- **Say only what you observed, and where.** "Not observed" is not "no".
- **Refuse, do not repair.** A refused action returns a code and changes nothing.
- **Agree independently.** Two separate computations must match before work is
  accepted; a disagreement names the entry to look at again.

## Ties

When two options are exactly equal, the earlier listed option wins. Write the rule
down before the numbers arrive.

## Honest limits

All prices and quotes are illustrative. The term-held boundary counts days, not
calendar dates. The margin model ignores fees and price paths.
