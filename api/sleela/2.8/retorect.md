# retorect

A perfectly repeating binary digit string of 5,050,446 digits beginning with `010101…` has probability \(2^{-5,050,446}\) of occurring as one particular string when each digit is independently and uniformly chosen from `0` and `1`. This is approximately one chance in \(10^{1,520,000}\). Strictly, because the pattern begins with zero, it is a fixed-length binary digit string rather than a conventional binary numeral with a nonzero leading digit. In decimal, the analogous fixed-length digit string `010101…` has probability \(10^{-5,050,446}\), or one chance in \(10^{5,050,446}\), under the simplifying assumption that each decimal digit is independently and uniformly chosen from `0` through `9`. Read as numerology, the repeated alternation and the length may be assigned symbolic meaning, but that interpretation is cultural or personal rather than a mathematical consequence of the pattern; the probabilities describe exact-string matching under the stated random-digit assumptions.
