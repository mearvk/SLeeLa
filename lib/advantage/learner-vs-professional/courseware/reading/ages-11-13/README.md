# Ages 11 to 13: Own It, Sell It, Count It Exactly

Readers for independent reading, 10 minutes each. Cards and game items stand in
for goods. No real trading is suggested; ask a grown-up about real money.

| # | reader | big idea | goes with |
|---|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | [Buy, Hold, Sell](./01-buy-hold-sell.md) | owning (long), oldest-first selling, profit | trading use cases 1 to 3 |
| 2 | [Averages With Leftovers](./02-averages-with-leftovers.md) | whole-number division and the remainder | trading use case 1 |
| 3 | [Euclid and the Biggest Shared Factor](./03-euclid-gcd.md) | gcd by a loop that must stop | trading use case 5 |
| 4 | [Not Observed Is Not No](./04-not-observed-is-not-no.md) | evidence, fields and honest answers | lessons 1 to 5 |

Each reader ends with questions and an answer key at the bottom.
