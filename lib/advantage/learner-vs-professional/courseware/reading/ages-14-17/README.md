# Ages 14 to 17: Risk, Proof, and Numbers That Lie

Readers for older students, 15 to 25 minutes each, with some code reading. These
describe how an illustrative trading book works, to teach reasoning and testing.
They are not advice and do not suggest trading real money. Anyone under 18
should talk to a parent or guardian before any real financial decision.

| # | reader | big idea | goes with |
|---|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | [Long and Short in Plain Words](./01-long-and-short.md) | profit, loss, and why risks differ | trading use cases 16 to 19 |
| 2 | [Euclid, Bezout and Exact Fills](./02-euclid-bezout-fills.md) | a proof you can check by multiplying | trading use case 6 |
| 3 | [Time Held, Fees and Term Lines](./03-time-fees-term.md) | days, costs and a strict boundary | trading use cases 8 to 12, 20 |
| 4 | [How We Know It Is Right](./04-how-we-know.md) | invariants, shadow models and mutations | trading use cases 14, 21, 22 |
| 5 | [Numbers That Lie](./05-numbers-that-lie.md) | an exact add but an inexact compare | `SLEuclid`, use case 15 |
