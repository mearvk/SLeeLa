# /lib/amazon — SLeeLa Workforce & Organization

Language-facing SLeeLa source for workforce planning, hiring, transition,
training/college, equipment, team relationships, and workplace-law checks.

This is an operations-management toolkit, not an autonomous employment judge.
Sensitive employment decisions require human review, documented criteria, and
applicable-law checks.

## Master Classes

- **SLHiringManager** — role/candidate fit and hiring workflow.
- **SLFiringManager** — termination workflow with mandatory human review.
- **SLWorkforceManager** — staffing, retention, reassignment and capacity.
- **SLCollegeManager** — internal education, courses and credentials.
- **SLFashionGearManager** — lawful uniform/fashion and equipment allocation.
- **SLGearManager** — equipment inventory and assignment.
- **SLFriendsManager** — healthy colleague/team connection support.
- **SLWorkplaceLaw** — jurisdiction/policy gate and escalation.
- **SLInterTrireManager** — succession, redeployment and retirement planning.
- **SLRetirementManager** — voluntary retirement and benefits-transition planning.
- **SLTalentManager** — skills, mentoring and development.
- **SLOrganizationManager** — teams, roles and organizational structure.

"InterTrire" is intentionally a SLeeLa term for intelligent transition:
people may retire, redeploy, mentor, or change roles based on lawful,
documented circumstances. Intelligence itself is never a reason to remove a
person.
