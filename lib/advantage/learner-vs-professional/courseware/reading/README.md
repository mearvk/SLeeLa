# Reading Material, Sorted by Age

Reading for every age, in order from youngest to oldest. Pick the band that fits
the reader, or start one band lower for a new topic. Each reader is short, has
questions, and (from age 11) an answer key.

| ages | folder | readers | style | about |
|------|--------|:-------:|-------|-------|
| 5 to 7 | [`ages-05-07`](./ages-05-07/README.md) | 4 | read aloud with a grown-up, one minute each | looking before saying, true or false, fair swaps, steps in order |
| 8 to 10 | [`ages-08-10`](./ages-08-10/README.md) | 4 | read alone or aloud, 3 to 5 minutes | a list with one place per thing, and/or/not, price layers, counting days |
| 11 to 13 | [`ages-11-13`](./ages-11-13/README.md) | 4 | independent reading, 10 minutes | buy/hold/sell, averages with leftovers, Euclid's loop, honest answers |
| 14 to 17 | [`ages-14-17`](./ages-14-17/README.md) | 5 | older students, code reading | long and short risk, Bezout, time and fees, testing, numbers that lie |
| 18 and up | [`ages-18-plus`](./ages-18-plus/README.md) | 5 | adults, 20 to 40 minutes | the whole system, position books, margin, correctness, assurance |

## One idea, five ages

| idea | 5 to 7 | 8 to 10 | 11 to 13 | 14 to 17 | 18 and up |
|------|--------|---------|----------|----------|-----------|
| say only what you saw | toy shelf | where does it live | not observed is not no | how we know | system on one page |
| fair and equal | fair swaps | peeling the onion | buy, hold, sell | long and short | position books |
| order matters | first, next, last | how old is it | averages with leftovers | time held and fees | correctness and cost |
| check your work | true or false | and, or, not | Euclid's loop | numbers that lie | assurance |

## Safe-use notes

- Everything is illustrative. No real market, order or price is used.
- Readers under 18 are told to ask a grown-up about real money.
- Nothing here is investment, tax, legal or regulatory advice.
