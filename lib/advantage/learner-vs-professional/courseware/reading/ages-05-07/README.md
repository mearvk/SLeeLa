# Ages 5 to 7: Look, Say, Swap

Short readers to read **with a grown-up**. Each one is about one minute aloud.
The big ideas: look before you say, true or false, fair swaps, steps in order.

| # | reader | big idea | goes with |
|---|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | [The Toy Shelf](./01-the-toy-shelf.md) | say only what you can see | children unit 1, lesson 1 |
| 2 | [True or False](./02-true-or-false.md) | a statement matches or it does not | children unit 1 |
| 3 | [Fair Swaps](./03-fair-swaps.md) | trading cards, counting, fair is equal | children unit 5 |
| 4 | [First, Next, Last](./04-first-next-last.md) | some jobs need an order | children unit 4, lesson 2 |

This is play with cards and toys. It is not about real money or real markets.
