AskForHelpMove (lib/gameplay/AskForHelpMove.sleela)

AskForHelpMove class extends GameMove

Move (b) — ask for help or new routes. A straight 50/50. Win the flip and the Inference Engine lets you LOOK at your Expected Future as it is being modeled, so you can see whether you are on schedule in your own custom game. Lose the flip and your Boss instead hands you a Detailed Report with some Game-Engineering moves already decided — and you pay for that information based on the Quality of your Person/Citizen. A concrete GameMove: override resolve() to flip, then either read the future or buy the report. The future it modeled and the report it bought are stashed on the move so the turn can show them. DiceRoll (lib/gameplay/DiceRoll.sleela)

DiceRoll class

Defines DiceRoll, the deterministic percent-roller at the heart of the gameplay package. Every "80% then 20%" and "50/50" in a turn is a DiceRoll. It is a seeded linear-congruential generator, so a given seed always replays the same game — the archive must repeat, even the luck. DiceRoll is a NATURAL / LOADABLE MODULE sharing the common module contract (load / loaded / moduleName / describe). It holds a single 32-bit-ish state word and advances it on every roll. No native randomness is used; "live play" is modeled by advancing the same deterministic stream. Family: gameplay.

GameMove (lib/gameplay/GameMove.sleela)
