# Where Does It Live?

A **registry** is a list that says where every thing is.

Our shelf:

| thing | lives in |
|-------|----------|
| red box | the shelf |
| blue box | the shelf |
| ball | red box |
| book | blue box |

Notice two rules:

1. **One place only.** The ball lives in the red box. It cannot also live in the
   blue box. If someone tries, the list says: "That thing already has a place."
2. **A place must be on the list first.** You cannot put the kite in the
   "green box" until the green box is on the list.

Things can sit inside things. A card is in a pocket, the pocket is on a jacket,
the jacket is in the closet. Read it as a chain: **card, pocket, jacket,
closet.** The chain always ends at the place we are looking at, which we call the
**field**.

If something is not on the list, say **"not observed"**. That means "I did not
see it here". It does **not** mean "it does not exist".

**Try it:** Make a list for your bedroom with 6 things. Check the two rules.
