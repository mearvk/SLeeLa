# /3 · BASIS — the Medium requirement for Workers, Work and Excellence — Workers

This is the **basis document** for `/3` [Facets](README.md). Where `/1` (Longs)
reasons about magnitudes that *run over time* and `/2` (Structures) reasons
about magnitudes that *stand*, `/3` reasons about **faces** — Facets. This
document states the **basis** on which the Facets set rests: the **Medium
requirement for Workers, Work and Excellence**, and — this document's scope — its
first pillar, **Workers**, read through the market's *moving* faces.

> **Discipline.** This is a *basis* — a stated foundation to be argued with, not
> a decree. Empirical claims are tagged to their source and period; everything
> else is an explicit modelling choice. `ASSUMPTION ≠ FACT`,
> `ASSOCIATION ≠ CAUSATION`.

## 1. What "the Medium" is here

In this repository a **medium** is a working substrate that is *assailable and
firm* — it can be worn or attacked, yet holds together and stays reconstructible
from its neighbors (see [`../../FILE.SYSTEM.md`](../../FILE.SYSTEM.md), "the
subject medium"). The Facets set reads that same working society **from its
moving faces**: not the stock of workers (that is `/2`'s Structures) but the
*flow* — how the market opens roles, lets workers move, and rewards their hours.
A medium is firm only if it can *keep moving* workers to work without seizing.

A medium therefore carries a **requirement**: the condition it must satisfy to
remain firm. For the working substrate that requirement has three pillars.

## 2. The Medium requirement — three pillars

| Pillar | The medium must guarantee | Read through Facets (this set) |
|---|---|---|
| **Workers** | that the society can *place and re-place* workers — the substrate stays fluid, not seized | job openings, quits (turnover faces) |
| **Work** | that the placed workers *put in real time* — hours that lay down extent | average weekly hours |
| **Excellence** | that the hours are *worth more over time* — quality rises | average hourly earnings (reward-of-work face) |

Together: **Workers · Work · Excellence** — the medium is firm when it is
*fluid*, *engaged*, and *rewarding*. This document covers the first pillar; the
other two are named here and reasoned about in the social model
([`models/SOCIAL_MODEL.md`](models/SOCIAL_MODEL.md)).

## 3. Workers — the requirement in scope

**Requirement (Workers).** *The medium is firm only while the market can place
and re-place workers freely — enough roles open, and enough movement between
them — so that the substrate is neither seized (no openings, no movement) nor
draining (movement without replacement).*

This is the "assailable and firm" property read through **flow**:

- **Firm** — enough openings exist that a lost worker (a quit, a retirement) can
  be replaced from a neighbor, the way the file medium reconstructs a lost cell
  from a cheap neighbor.
- **Assailable** — workers *do* move (a non-zero quits rate); the substrate is
  not frozen. Healthy churn moves workers toward better-fitting work so long as
  the openings to receive them exist.

### The Workers facets (evidence)

The requirement is checked against the real, sourced **Workers** facets in
[`data/social-labor-facets.json`](data/social-labor-facets.json):

| Facet | Value | Period | What it tells the requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job openings level | 6.9 million | 2024/25 | how many roles stand ready to receive workers |
| Job openings rate | 4.2% | 2024/25 | openings as a share of the filled-plus-open market |
| Quits rate | 2.0% | 2024 | how freely workers move (the assailable face) |
| Average weekly hours (private) | 34.3 | 2024 | the engaged-time face (Work) |

**Reading (a model, not a verdict).** With millions of openings ready to receive
workers (a firm face) and a moderate quits rate that keeps workers moving toward
better fit (an assailable face), the working substrate reads as *fluid* by this
basis — able to place and re-place without seizing. This is an interpretation of
published figures over a stated period, not a causal or normative claim about
any person.

## 4. How Workers connects to Work and Excellence

The Workers pillar is a **precondition**, not the whole requirement:

- **Workers → Work.** Only a market that keeps placing workers can turn them into
  engaged hours; openings and movement are the base on which hours (the Work
  pillar) rest.
- **Work → Excellence.** Work becomes Excellence when each hour is *worth more* —
  read here through **average hourly earnings**: a rising reward per hour is the
  market's face of work done better. Excellence is work that *lengthens* (adds
  durable, better-rewarded extent) rather than *substitutes* (churns without
  added worth) — the same distinction the `/1` assumptions layer draws.

So the basis reads top to bottom: **a fluid market of Workers** makes **engaged
Work** possible, and **Work worth more per hour** is **Excellence**. Where `/2`
tests the pillars through the *stock* of a working society, `/3` tests them
through its *flow*.
