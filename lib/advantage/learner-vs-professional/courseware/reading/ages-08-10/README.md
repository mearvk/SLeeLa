# Ages 8 to 10: Where Is It? What Does It Cost?

Readers for a young reader to read alone or aloud. Each takes 3 to 5 minutes.

| # | reader | big idea | goes with |
|---|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | [Where Does It Live?](./01-where-does-it-live.md) | a list where every thing has one place | children units 1 and 3, lessons 1 and 5 |
| 2 | [And, Or, Not](./02-and-or-not.md) | combining facts, and checking with a friend | children unit 2, lesson 4 |
| 3 | [Peeling the Onion](./03-peeling-the-onion.md) | price + fee - coupon, one layer at a time | children unit 5, lesson 6 |
| 4 | [How Old Is It?](./04-how-old-is-it.md) | counting days, oldest first | trading use cases 8 and 9 |

Everything here uses toys, cards and coins. Real money is for grown-ups.
