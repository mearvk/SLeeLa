# The Toy Shelf

Here is a shelf. It has two boxes and one block.

- The **red box** has a **ball** in it.
- The **blue box** has a **book** in it.
- The **block** sits on the shelf by itself.

This one shelf is our **field**. A field is the place we are allowed to look.

Point and say:

- "The ball is in the **red** box." You can see it. That is true!
- "The kite is on the shelf." You do **not** see a kite.
  Do not say "no kite in the world". Say: **"I do not see a kite on this shelf."**

Every thing has **one** place. The ball cannot be in two boxes at once.

**Try it:** Make your own shelf with a shoebox. Put 3 toys on it.
Close your eyes, then open them and tell a grown-up where each toy is.
