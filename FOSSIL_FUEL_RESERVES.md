# SLeeLa — FOSSIL_FUEL_RESERVES.md

## Russia's published fossil-fuel reserves manual (RF2013)

This note records, as a sourced fact, the principal **published manual** governing
how oil and gas reserves are classified and reported in Russia. It is a
descriptive reference entry, consistent with the project's data-integrity rule:
the claims below are tied to cited sources, not inferred.

### Terminology clarification

The **pre-1917 Russian Empire (Tsarist)** did **not** publish a modern
petroleum-reserves classification manual — systematic reserves classification of
this kind did not exist in that era. The authoritative manual is a **Russian
Federation** standard with a lineage running back through the **Soviet/USSR**
state-reserve system. Where this document says "Russia," it refers to that
modern federal standard and its Soviet-era predecessors, **not** the Tsarist
empire.

### The manual: RF2013

**Full name:** Reserves and Resources Classification of Oil and Combustible
(Fuel) Gases of the Russian Federation, 2013 — commonly abbreviated **"RF2013."**

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Approving authority | Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment of the Russian Federation |
| Approval instrument | Order No. 477, dated **1 November 2013** |
| Justice Ministry registration | **31 December 2013**, registration No. **30943** |
| Enacted (in force) | **1 January 2016** |
| Scope | Uniform principles and guidelines for estimating and reporting reserves and resources of **oil, fuel gases** (free gas, gas-cap gas, gas dissolved in oil) and **gas condensate** |
| Reserve categories | **A, B1, B2** (with off-balance variants **A\***, **B1\***, **B2\***), based primarily on degree of geological knowledge |

### Why it qualifies as a "strong" manual

- It is the **legally mandated state standard** — approved by ministerial order and
  registered with the Ministry of Justice — not merely an industry guideline.
- It underpins the official **State Balance of Mineral Reserves** (the state
  inventory of petroleum reserves and resources), administered through
  **Rosnedra** (Federal Agency for Subsoil Use) and the **GKZ** (State Commission
  on Mineral Reserves).
- Its classification is grounded in **geological confidence**, and a formal
  **bridging document** maps RF2013 categories to the United Nations Framework
  Classification (UNFC), giving it recognized international interoperability.

### Historical lineage

RF2013 replaced earlier **Soviet/USSR classifications** (notably the 1983 Soviet
classification, itself descended from earlier Soviet systems). The state-reserve
manual therefore forms a continuous line from the USSR to the present Russian
Federation — but, as noted above, not from the pre-1917 Russian Empire.
