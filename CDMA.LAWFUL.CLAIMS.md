# CDMA Lawful Claims Registry

**Purpose:** a concise, evidence-led registry for lawful claims, practical help, and the points where life and CMDA-related concerns intersect. This document is a project record and general information, not legal advice or a determination that any claim is valid.

## Scope and naming

- **CDMA** is the registry label used by this project. Its full expansion, sponsoring body, jurisdiction, and authority must be supplied by the project owner before the registry represents them as established facts.
- **CMDA** is preserved as a separate term because it may refer to a different body or concept. Do not silently treat CDMA and CMDA as interchangeable.
- **“Norfolks that Can!”** is recorded as the intended community context/name supplied for this work. Do not infer official status, membership, endorsement, or legal authority from the name alone.

## Registry entry format

Each claim should record:

1. **Claim ID and date**
2. **Claim summary** — concise, neutral wording
3. **Claimant or responsible contact** — keep personal details private unless disclosure is necessary and authorized
4. **Jurisdiction and relevant authority** — identify the applicable location and official source
5. **Evidence and provenance** — documents, dates, source links, and what each source actually establishes
6. **Life impact** — safety, dignity, welfare, accessibility, family/community impact, or other stated effects
7. **CDMA/CMDA relationship** — identify the specific policy, service, organization, or issue; mark unknowns as unknown
8. **Status** — unreviewed, evidence requested, under review, referred, resolved, or closed
9. **Next action and review date**

## Lawful and responsible handling

- Separate verified facts, allegations, interpretations, and open questions.
- Do not publish private or sensitive information without a lawful basis and a clear need.
- Do not use the registry to threaten, harass, expose, or target individuals.
- Preserve original evidence and record corrections; do not alter evidence to fit a conclusion.
- Refer urgent threats to life or safety to the relevant emergency service. Seek qualified legal advice for specific rights, deadlines, remedies, or proceedings.
- A registry entry is not proof of wrongdoing, a legal finding, or authorization to act against anyone.

## Life and CMDA: where they intersect

The intersection should be recorded only when a concrete connection is identified—for example, a policy or service affecting a person's safety, access to help, care, housing, work, community participation, or other real-life outcome. Describe the effect, identify the supporting source, and distinguish observed impact from opinion. If the meaning or role of “CMDA” is not verified, mark the relationship **unconfirmed** and request the official name or reference.

## Review standard

A reviewer should be able to answer: **What is claimed? What evidence supports it? Which authority or policy applies? Who may be affected? What safe, lawful next step is proportionate?** If any answer is unknown, record that uncertainty instead of filling the gap with assumptions.

*Maintain this file as a transparent index of claims and help pathways—not as a substitute for official records, professional advice, or due process.*
