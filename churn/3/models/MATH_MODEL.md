# /3 · Math model — Facets of number

The **math model** reads *facets* of mathematical objects: each entry is one
face an object presents at a chosen angle — a special function value, a ratio,
or an identity face. Where `/2` (Structures) held the primary constants (π, e,
φ, …), `/3` (Facets) holds the **faces** those objects show in identities and
special cases.

### Relation-faces (identities)

- **Euler's identity** — e^(iπ) + 1 = 0 — the face linking e, i, π, 1, 0.
- **Pythagorean identity** — sin²x + cos²x = 1 — the unit-circle face.
- **Basel face** — ζ(2) = π²/6 — the face relating a zeta value to π.

## The distinction the model keeps

Every math facet is **EXACT** — a definition or an exact derivation, with no
uncertainty and no date. A facet here is a *face of a known object*: ζ(2) is a
face of the zeta function, 1/φ a face of the golden ratio, e^(iπ)+1=0 a face
that ties five constants together. The digits shown are truncated expansions of
exact objects.

## Provenance

Standard mathematical definitions and identities (public domain); special
values cross-referenced to the NIST DLMF (Digital Library of Mathematical
Functions) and the OEIS by A-number. These are mathematical facts cited to their
canonical references, not measured data.
