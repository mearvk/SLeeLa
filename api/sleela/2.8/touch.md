# touch

A perfectly repeating binary digit string of 5,050,446 digits beginning with `101010…` has probability \\(2^{-5,050,446}\\) of occurring as one particular string when each digit is independently and uniformly chosen from `0` and `1`. This is approximately one chance in \\(10^{1,520,000}\\). Because the pattern begins with one, it is also a valid conventional fixed-length binary numeral. In decimal, the analogous fixed-length digit string `101010…` has probability \\(10^{-5,050,446}\\), or one chance in \\(10^{5,050,446}\\), under the simplifying assumption that each decimal digit is independently and uniformly chosen from `0` through `9`. Read as numerology, the repeated alternation and the length may be assigned symbolic meaning, but that interpretation is cultural or personal rather than a mathematical consequence of the pattern; the probabilities describe exact-string matching under the stated random-digit assumptions.
