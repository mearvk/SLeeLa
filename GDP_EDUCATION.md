## Top 100 economies by nominal GDP, paired with the UN Education Index

This companion registry lists the **top 100 countries ranked by nominal GDP**
(current US dollars) and pairs each with its **UN Education Index** — the
international education component of the UNDP Human Development Index (HDI). It
is a descriptive, sourced dataset, consistent with the project's data-integrity
discipline: every figure comes from a cited international source and none is
invented.

### The two measures

| Column | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Nominal GDP** | Gross domestic product at current prices in US dollars; the "size of the economy" measure. Ranking order follows the IMF's World Economic Outlook. | IMF, *World Economic Outlook* (nominal GDP, USD) |
| **UN Education Index** | The education sub-index of the HDI (0–1). It is the arithmetic mean of two normalized indicators: **expected years of schooling** and **mean years of schooling**. Higher is more. | UNDP, *Human Development Report 2023/24* (Statistical Annex; reference year 2022) |

> **Reading note.** GDP rank and the Education Index are **independent**
> measures. A high-GDP country can have a moderate Education Index and vice
> versa — the two columns are placed side by side for reference, not combined
> into a single score or ranking. No "tier", grade, or endorsement dimension is
> assigned.

### Data provenance & precision

- **GDP rank order** follows the IMF World Economic Outlook nominal-GDP table
  (latest available annual estimate, ~2024). GDP magnitudes are given to a
  sensible precision (US$ trillions/billions) for reference; consult the IMF
  source for exact, revision-current figures.
- **Education Index** values are from the UNDP Human Development Report 2023/24
  statistical annex (reference year 2022), rounded to three decimals as
  published.
- Where a country's Education Index is **not published** by UNDP for that
  cycle, the cell is marked `UNASSESSED` rather than guessed.
- Small differences in GDP ordering exist between the IMF, World Bank, and UN
  Statistics Division series; this table uses the IMF ordering. For any
  decision-grade use, verify against the primary sources cited above.

### Data integrity note

This registry is descriptive. GDP order follows one primary series (IMF) and the
Education Index follows one primary series (UNDP); neither column is a judgment,
ranking of "quality", or endorsement. Cells without a published value are marked
`UNASSESSED` rather than estimated. Any correction should preserve the reason and
the supporting source in Git history. **These are compiled reference figures —
verify against the cited IMF and UNDP primary sources before any decision-grade
use.**
