# Fair Swaps

Mia has **3** star stickers. Sam has **3** moon stickers.

They **swap** one star for one moon. Now Mia has 2 stars and 1 moon. Sam has
2 moons and 1 star. Each still has **3** stickers.

A swap is **fair** when both people are happy and both can count the same
things going each way.

Count it out loud:

- Mia gives **1**, gets **1**.
- Sam gives **1**, gets **1**.

If Mia gave **2** stickers and got **1**, would that be fair? Ask Mia!
It could be fair only if she wants it that way, and she says so.

**Try it:** Swap three small toys with a grown-up. Say what each person gave and
got. Do both people agree it was fair?

This is play. Real money is for grown-ups to handle.
