# Intel iAPX 432 Specification

The iAPX 432 combines:

- object-oriented architectural state;
- capability/protection-oriented addressing;
- descriptor-based objects;
- instruction decoding and execution through a complex architectural control model;
- memory-management and protection facilities;
- interrupt and fault handling;
- microcode-assisted implementation.

The 432 is not modeled as an ordinary flat register-file RISC or as an extension of x86.
