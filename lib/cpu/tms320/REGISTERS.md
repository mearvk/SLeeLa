# Texas Instruments TMS320 Registers

The register model is profile-driven. Depending on the selected family member it can include:

- accumulator and product registers;
- auxiliary/address registers;
- status and control registers;
- program counter and stack state;
- repeat/loop counters;
- floating-point registers on applicable generations.

SLeeLa does not claim a single register map for every TMS320 generation. The selected profile must supply the exact register inventory and widths.
