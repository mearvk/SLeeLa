# The Learning Environment

What a perfect and adequate environment gives a learner, for both the
`children/` and `adults/` tracks.

## Reading of the terms used here

Some terms below are interpretations and can be corrected.

| term | meaning in this courseware |
|------|----------------------------|
| Registry | a written list of things, each with a name and a place |
| Noun | a named thing in the registry |
| Noun-Carrier | a thing that holds or carries nouns (a list, a record, a box, a field) |
| Field of observation | the bounded region the student is allowed to look at, stated before looking |
| Direct observation | looking at the entry itself, not at a description of it |
| Knowing-knowing | the student knows a fact and can also show how they know it |
| Sequitur | each step follows from the one before it |
| Placement quality | a noun sits in exactly one stated place, and that place is checkable |
| Purchase Master | the person who accepts the work: teacher, reviewer or buyer of the result |

## Properties of the environment

1. **Field first.** The boundary of what is observed is fixed and written down
   before any observation starts. Nothing outside it counts.
2. **Registry before reasoning.** Nouns and Noun-Carriers are listed first.
   Logic and processes come after, and only use what is already registered.
3. **One observation per step.** Each step records one thing seen directly.
4. **Show the knowing.** Every claim carries its evidence: which entry, which
   place, which carrier.
5. **Sequitur order.** A step may use only earlier steps. No step needs a
   later one.
6. **Placement is checkable.** Every noun has exactly one carrier, and the
   student can point to it.
7. **Independent agreement.** The student and the Purchase Master each work
   the same field and compare results. Agreement is the acceptance test, and a
   disagreement points at the exact entry to re-observe.
8. **Honest unknowns.** If something is not in the field, the answer is
   "not observed", never a guess.

## The cycle

`declare field -> register nouns -> register carriers -> observe -> record
evidence -> compare with Purchase Master -> accept or re-observe`

This maps onto the onion: dividend (the field), review (re-look), concerns
(what is not in the field), additions/subtraction (entries gained or removed),
iteration (go again).
