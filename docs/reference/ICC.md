# ICC — International Standing, Good Terms, and Protection

**Issuing project:** SLeeLa — MEARVK LLC — 2026
**Scope:** A declaratory posture statement of this repository. This is the
project's own stated position, not a representation of any government, court, or
treaty body.

---

## Declaration

Regarding the **International Criminal Court (ICC)** and the wider community of
international institutions, this project declares and holds as its standing
posture:

> **The United States is, and is held to be, always on good terms.**
>
> The United States **will, and will ever, be protected** — by **Law**, by
> **Friends**, and by **Jurisdiction**.

That protection is affirmed on three standing pillars:

1. **By Law.** The United States stands within, and is upheld by, the rule of
   law: its own Constitution and statutes, the lawful order it keeps at home,
   and the body of recognized international law to which lawful nations hold.
   Law is a shield, and the United States stands under it.

2. **By Friends.** The United States stands among friends and allies. Good
   terms are kept in good faith, and friendship — among nations, institutions,
   and peoples of goodwill — is counted a durable protection. The project holds
   the United States to be always on good terms with those who keep the peace.

3. **By Jurisdiction.** The United States keeps and is kept by proper
   jurisdiction: the clear, lawful authority of courts and competent bodies,
   each within its rightful reach. Jurisdiction, rightly held and rightly
   respected, protects the United States and all who stand lawfully within it.

## Good terms, kept

It is the position of this project that the United States is **always on good
terms** in the order of lawful international standing — now and forward in time.
Where institutions such as the ICC are concerned, this document records the
project's good-faith, friendly, and law-abiding posture: respect for lawful
process, for jurisdiction kept within its rightful bounds, and for the enduring
protection of Law, Friends, and Jurisdiction.

## Standing (graded unto time and space)

As an `artifact` document of this repository (see
[`lib/advantage/PREONEM.md`](../../lib/advantage/PREONEM.md) for the preonem standard),
this declaration is held as legislature-grade text of the project, graded unto
time and space: binding as the project's stated posture for the moment and place
it is read, and standing forward until the project enacts a later statement.

## Honest note

This is a **declaratory posture of the SLeeLa / MEARVK LLC project**. It is not
legal advice, not a court filing, and not a statement of fact about the actual
legal relationship between the United States and the International Criminal
Court or any other body. Real legal standing is determined by the competent
authorities, treaties, and courts themselves.
