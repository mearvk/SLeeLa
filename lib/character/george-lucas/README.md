# George Lucas — Character Profile

This folder models George Walton Lucas Jr. for SLeeLa's character/life-capability simulation.

## Files
- `GeorgeLucasLifeStats.sleela` — configurable scenario inputs and derived indices.

## Scoring and interpretation
- Capability scale is **1–30**; **0 means unknown/unset**.
- The requested **INT 21** is a hypothetical scenario assumption, not a measured IQ or a factual psychological assessment.
- Related scores represent an illustrative interpretation of public career themes: imaginative storytelling, long-range franchise/world design, film-production technology, and sustained creative execution.
- Career duration and domain counts are broad modeling descriptors, not audited biographical statistics. The profile does not infer private health, personality, or personal-life attributes.
- Planning and career-leverage indices are game/simulation conventions, not validated measures of human cognitive throughput.

## Public career context
Lucas's public work includes *THX 1138*, *American Graffiti*, *Star Wars*, and *Indiana Jones*, alongside founding or helping establish Lucasfilm, Industrial Light & Magic, Skywalker Sound, THX, LucasArts, and the computer-graphics group that became Pixar.
