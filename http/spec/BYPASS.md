# BYPASS.md — Secular Civic Wisdom of 391 World Entries

**Status:** Guiding cultural and civic knowledge document  
**Scope:** 391 current country/territory entries from ISO 3166-1 plus historical countries and polities, presented as a project taxonomy.  

## Purpose

This document treats **secular religion** as a metaphor for shared civic commitments rather than as a new religion. It means the practical values that people can hold across religious and non-religious differences: human dignity, truth-seeking, learning, responsibility, justice, peace, stewardship, freedom of conscience, and care for future generations.

There is no universally accepted international list of “391 countries.” The United Nations currently has 193 Member States, while UN statistical geography includes countries and areas for statistical purposes. Accordingly, this document deliberately uses **391 world entries** rather than claiming that international law recognizes 391 sovereign states. citeturn0search3turn0search10

## Method

Each entry contains one concise paragraph. The paragraph is a **secular wisdom lens**, not a claim that every person in a country shares the same values. Religious, ethnic, political, linguistic, and cultural diversity within each entry is respected. Historical entries are included to preserve continuity and memory, not to imply present sovereignty.
