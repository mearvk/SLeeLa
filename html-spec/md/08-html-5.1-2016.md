## Summary

HTML 5.1 was the W3C's first incremental update after HTML5, intended to capture features that had become stable and interoperable since 2014, and to remove things that hadn't. It was produced from the `w3c/html` deliverables repository.

## Notable changes

- Added the `<picture>` element plus `srcset`/`sizes` on `<img>` for **responsive images**
- Added `<details>` / `<summary>` for native disclosure widgets
- `<menu>` / context-menu work (later largely reversed)
- `rev` and other long-obsolete bits cleaned up; many conformance clarifications
- Dropped features that lacked two interoperable implementations

## Significance

Established the W3C's cadence of periodic "5.x" snapshots of a continuously-evolving language — a model that would soon be resolved in favor of the WHATWG Living Standard.
