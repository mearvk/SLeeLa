# Ages 18 and Up: Reading Guide

Readers for adults, 20 to 40 minutes each. They describe an illustrative
trading-book library built to teach exact reasoning. Nothing here is investment,
tax, legal or regulatory advice, and no order is ever placed.

| # | reader | big idea | goes with |
|---|--------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | [The Whole System on One Page](./01-the-system-on-one-page.md) | what exists, in what order to read it | everything |
| 2 | [Position Books: Long, Short and the Mirror](./02-position-books.md) | accounting identities and refusal as a design | use cases 1 to 4, 16 to 22 |
| 3 | [Margin, Exactly](./03-margin-exactly.md) | ceiling arithmetic and the search for the fewest shares | use cases 23 to 26 |
| 4 | [Correctness and Cost](./04-correctness-and-cost.md) | invariants, termination, growth | adults units 3 and 4 |
| 5 | [Assurance and What Went Wrong](./05-assurance-and-what-went-wrong.md) | the layers of checking and the review log | `ASSURANCE.md` |
| 6 | [Single-Trade Sensitivity, and Why Daily Rates Like These Are Not Real](./06-single-trade-sensitivity.md) | honest arithmetic on a hypothetical "good day" return and "microtrade advantage" multiple, and why compounding either one shows the assumption cannot be sustained | `SLLongBook` |
| 7 | [A Batch Total Is Not a Per-Trade Rate](./07-batch-vs-per-trade.md) | 20% over a 2,000-trade batch means 1 bp per trade, not 20% per trade repeated 2,000 times (which reaches 400x capital with no compounding at all) | `SLBatchReturn`, use case 27 |
