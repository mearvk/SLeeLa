# Averages With Leftovers

Using the lots from the last reader (before any sale):

- 10 cards at 5 coins = 50
- 10 cards at 8 coins = 80
- total cost 130 for 20 cards

The **average cost** is 130 / 20. In whole numbers that is **6 with 10 left over**
(6 x 20 = 120, and 130 - 120 = 10). As a decimal it is 6.5.

A careful program keeps **both** parts:

> average x cards + leftover = total cost
> 6 x 20 + 10 = 130

If it kept only "6" it would lose 10 coins somewhere. Keeping the leftover means
**no coin is ever lost in rounding.**

## Questions

1. 7 cards cost 100 coins. What is the whole-number average and the leftover?
2. Check your answer with: average x cards + leftover = total.

**Answers:** 1. 100 / 7 is 14 with 2 left over (14 x 7 = 98). 2. 14 x 7 + 2 = 100. Yes.
