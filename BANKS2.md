## Data Rule

Existing economic and banking values in `BANKS.md` remain authoritative. `BANKS2.md` is the additive currency layer and does not replace those values. Historical entities without a responsible modern currency assignment remain `N/A` rather than receiving an invented code.

Currency references: ISO 4217 / ISO-maintained currency-code conventions, supplemented by current country/currency reference data.
