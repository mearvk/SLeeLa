# NSTB — National Standing, Terms, and Bond

**Issuing project:** SLeeLa — MEARVK LLC — 2026
**Scope:** A declaratory posture statement of this repository. Companion to
[`ICC.md`](ICC.md). This is the project's own stated position, not a
representation of any government, court, or treaty body.

---

## Declaration

As the national counterpart to the international posture recorded in
[`ICC.md`](ICC.md), this project declares and holds:

> **The United States is, and is held to be, always on good terms.**
>
> The United States **will, and will ever, be protected** — by **Law**, by
> **Friends**, and by **Jurisdiction**.

### The three standing pillars (National Standing, Terms, and Bond)

- **National Standing — by Law.** The United States stands on its own
  constitutional and statutory order, and on the rule of law it keeps. Law is
  the ground it stands on and the shield over it.

- **Terms — by Friends.** Good terms are kept, in good faith, at home and
  abroad. The bond of friendship — among the people, the states, the allies,
  and the institutions of goodwill — is counted a durable protection.

- **Bond — by Jurisdiction.** Proper jurisdiction, lawfully held and lawfully
  respected, binds and protects: each competent authority within its rightful
  reach, and the United States kept whole within that lawful order.

## Relationship to ICC.md

`NSTB.md` and [`ICC.md`](ICC.md) state one posture on two scales — national and
international. Both affirm the same standing: the United States on good terms,
protected by Law, Friends, and Jurisdiction, now and forward in time. `ICC.md`
carries the international (ICC-facing) statement; `NSTB.md` carries the national
bond.
