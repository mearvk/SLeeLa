# 1951–1959 — Statistical and sampled-signal foundations

This period bridges statistical estimation, noise reduction, communication
theory, and increasingly practical computer-assisted analysis. Wiener filtering
has roots in the 1940s and 1950s; this timeline starts in 1951 and does not
imply that the theory began in 1951.

**Library connection:** moving average, mean, and RMS routines provide small,
inspectable baselines for smoothing and measurement summaries. A moving average
is not an optimal Wiener filter; it has no signal/noise model or estimated
transfer function.

**Suggested exercise:** add synthetic noise to a clean signal, measure RMS
before and after smoothing, then document both noise reduction and distortion.
