# /4 · BASIS — the Medium requirement for Workers, Work and Excellence — Workers

This is the **basis document** for `/4` [Areas](README.md). Where `/1` (Longs)
reasons about magnitudes that *run over time*, `/2` (Structures) about
magnitudes that *stand*, and `/3` (Facets) about *faces*, `/4` reasons about
**extents** — Areas. This document states the **basis** on which the Areas set
rests: the **Medium requirement for Workers, Work and Excellence**, and — this
document's scope — its first pillar, **Workers**, read through the **ground** the
working substrate spans.

> **Discipline.** This is a *basis* — a stated foundation to be argued with, not
> a decree. Empirical claims are tagged to their source and period; everything
> else is an explicit modelling choice. `ASSUMPTION ≠ FACT`,
> `ASSOCIATION ≠ CAUSATION`.

## 1. What "the Medium" is here

In this repository a **medium** is a working substrate that is *assailable and
firm* — it can be worn or attacked, yet holds together and stays reconstructible
from its neighbors (see [`../../FILE.SYSTEM.md`](../../FILE.SYSTEM.md), "the
subject medium"). The Areas set reads that same working society **from the ground
it spans**: not the stock of workers (`/2`) nor the market's flow (`/3`) but the
**real extent** the medium occupies and how densely it fills it. A medium is
firm only if it is *spread over enough ground* and *filled firmly enough* to bear
load without seizing.

A medium therefore carries a **requirement**: the condition it must satisfy to
remain firm. For the working substrate that requirement has three pillars.

## 2. The Medium requirement — three pillars

| Pillar | The medium must guarantee | Read through Areas (this set) |
|---|---|---|
| **Workers** | that the substrate *spans real ground* and *fills it firmly* — neither depopulated nor over-packed | land area, population density |
| **Work** | that the ground is *put to use* — extent that carries output, not idle | area under productive use |
| **Excellence** | that each unit of ground *yields more over time* — density of value rises | output per area, value density |

Together: **Workers · Work · Excellence** — the medium is firm when it is
*grounded*, *used*, and *yielding*. This document covers the first pillar; the
other two are named here and reasoned about in the social model
([`models/SOCIAL_MODEL.md`](models/SOCIAL_MODEL.md)).

## 3. Workers — the requirement in scope

**Requirement (Workers).** *The medium is firm only while the working substrate
spans a real extent and fills it firmly enough — enough land, filled at a
sustainable density — so that it is neither depopulated (too sparse to bear
load) nor over-packed (too dense to recover from a loss).*

This is the "assailable and firm" property read through **ground**:

- **Firm** — the substrate is filled densely enough that a lost cell (a
  departed worker, an emptied region) can be reconstructed from a neighbor, the
  way the file medium reconstructs a lost cell from a cheap neighbor.
- **Assailable** — the ground is *not* uniformly saturated; there is room for
  workers to move and regions to grow. Open extent is what keeps the substrate
  changeable rather than seized.

### The Workers areas (evidence)

The requirement is checked against the real, sourced **Areas** in
[`data/social-density-us.json`](data/social-density-us.json):

| Area | Value | What it tells the requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Land area | 9,147,420 km² | the extent the medium spans — the room it has |
| Resident population | 341,784,857 | how many fill it |
| Population density | 37.4 /km² | how firmly the ground is filled |

**Reading (a model, not a verdict).** With a large land extent filled at a
moderate density (~37 persons/km²), the working substrate reads as *grounded and
firm* by this basis — spread over ample room (assailable: space to grow and
move) yet filled firmly enough to bear load and reconstruct a loss from a
neighbor. This is an interpretation of published figures, not a causal or
normative claim about any person or place.

## 4. How Workers connects to Work and Excellence

The Workers pillar is a **precondition**, not the whole requirement:

- **Workers → Work.** Only a grounded, firmly-filled substrate can put its
  extent to use; land and density are the base on which productive use (the Work
  pillar) rests.
- **Work → Excellence.** Work becomes Excellence when each unit of ground
  *yields more* over time — the value-density face. Excellence is ground that
  *lengthens* (rising yield per area) rather than *substitutes* (spreading thin
  without added yield) — the same distinction the `/1` assumptions layer draws.

So the basis reads top to bottom: **enough grounded Workers** make **used
ground** possible, and **ground that yields more per unit** is **Excellence**.
Across the four sets, the same requirement is tested four ways — over *time*
(`/1`), by *stock* (`/2`), by *flow* (`/3`), and over *ground* (`/4`).
