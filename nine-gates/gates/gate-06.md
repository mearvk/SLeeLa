## Motif

Rolling waves inside a framing circle, with a small moon set above them — the pull that raises and
lowers the water.

## Meaning

The sixth gate is **forces beyond your control**. After the keystone's effort comes the hard
lesson that not everything yields to will: tides rise and fall on their own schedule, and the only
skill is reading them and timing your move. It stands for patience, for waiting out conditions
that cannot be forced, and for the humility to work with a rhythm you did not set.
