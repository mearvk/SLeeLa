# SLeeLa Brand Guide

**Brand line:** The Now. The Ingredient.  
**Identity:** SLeeLa — language, compiler, runtime, libraries, and tools.

## Logo
- Wide placement: [wordmark SVG](../images/brand/sleela-wordmark.svg).
- Square placement: [standalone mark SVG](../images/brand/sleela-mark.svg).
- Keep clear space equal to at least one leaf segment. Do not stretch, rotate, bevel, glow, or redraw the mark.
- Minimum digital size: wordmark 180 px wide; mark 24 px. Use monochrome at very small sizes.
- Preferred backgrounds are charcoal or white with accessible contrast.

## Color tokens
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| SLeeLa Green | `#00D68F` | Primary accent, links, active states |
| Deep Green | `#008F62` | Secondary accent |
| Charcoal | `#0B0F0F` | Dark backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light surfaces and reversed wordmark |
| Slate | `#A0A9AD` | Secondary copy |

Use green as a signal, not as a wall of color. Never rely on color alone to communicate state.

## Typography and voice
Use Montserrat or a similar geometric sans-serif for display headings; use a legible system sans-serif for prose and monospace for code. Write professionally, directly, and specifically. Prefer “tested on Linux” over “works everywhere.” Label capabilities **implemented**, **tested**, **experimental**, or **planned** where status matters. Avoid unsupported speed, security, standards, and compatibility claims.

## Product family
Language, compiler, runtime/SLVM, libraries, connectors, terminal, UI, and documentation share one identity. Use descriptive sub-product names and the common mark. Create a distinct sub-brand only for a stable product with its own audience and release lifecycle.

## Merchandise
The merchandise catalog is design concept art, not proof that products are manufactured or for sale. Prefer durable black/white bases, restrained green accents, small chest marks, and a one-color variant for embroidery and engraving. See [merchandise](../merchandise/README.md).
