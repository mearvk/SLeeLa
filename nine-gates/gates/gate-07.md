## Motif

A single flame rising above a small, settled stack of fuel — not a bonfire, just one persistent
tongue of fire with a brighter core.

## Meaning

The seventh gate is **what survives**. After the tide has done its work, what remains is a small,
stubborn spark — the core motive or conviction that outlasts setbacks and keeps the whole thing
alive on low fuel. It stands for resilience without grandeur: not the roaring start but the quiet
ember that can be fanned back into flame when conditions allow.
