# Unit 5 (Children): Peeling the Onion

**You will learn:** to work a problem one layer at a time, and to check your
answer with a helper. **Use with:** [lessons 5 and 6](../series/README.md).

## Layers

An onion has layers. One layer follows the one before it.

| layer | what you do |
|-------|-------------|
| dividend | write the starting number |
| review | look again to make sure it is right |
| concerns | what could go wrong, or what you did not look at |
| additions | add extras |
| subtraction | take things away |
| iteration | check again |

## The toy shop

Shop A sells a kite for **12 coins**. There is a **2 coin** bag fee. A coupon
takes **3 coins** off.

1. Start: 12 coins. Add the bag fee. Now?
2. Take off the coupon. Now?
3. What is the final price at Shop A?

Shop B sells the same kite for **11 coins** with a **2 coin** bag fee and no coupon.

4. What is the final price at Shop B?
5. Which shop is cheaper, and by how much?

## Check with a helper

6. Your friend says Shop A is 14 coins. They forgot the coupon. What do you do?
   (Look at the coupon again; do not just copy.)
7. Shop C is not on your list. Can you say its price?

## Boxes inside boxes

A kite shop has a **price list**. The price list holds **Shop A**. Shop A holds
the **kite price**.

8. Say who holds the kite price. Say who holds that. (The kite price is in Shop A,
   which is in the price list.) This is a chain back to the field.

Run lesson 5 (boxes inside boxes) and lesson 6 (prices) with a grown-up.

## Answer key

1. 14 coins. 2. 11 coins. 3. 11 coins.
4. 13 coins (11 + 2).
5. Shop A is cheaper, by 2 coins (11 vs 13).
6. Look at the coupon again, then fix the number. Do not just change to match.
7. No. Shop C is not on your list, so it is "not observed". The Instructor says
   the same: it does not guess.
8. The kite price is held by Shop A. Shop A is held by the price list.
   The price list is the field.
