## Education and social-development context

Record statistics by **country or subnational population**, not as inherent traits of speakers of this language. A language is not a country or a homogeneous population, and speakers may be multilingual or live across many countries.

| Indicator | Definition / preferred measure | Value, geography, year, source |
|---|---|---|
| Tertiary education | Adult tertiary attainment (state age range and level) | Not yet researched |
| Life-course / age structure | Median age and age distribution; avoid labeling people or cultures as “mature” | Not yet researched |
| Well-being | Life satisfaction or a validated well-being measure; “loveliness” is subjective and is not a population statistic | Not yet researched |
| Fertility | Total fertility rate (births per woman), with year; do not confuse with births per capita | Not yet researched |
| Savings | Gross domestic savings (% of GDP) or household saving rate, clearly distinguished | Not yet researched |

## Method and cautions

- Speaker rank is a language-reach measure, not a measure of social aptitude, intelligence, maturity, beauty, or worth.
- Country-level indicators describe the relevant country and year; they must not be attributed to every speaker of this language.
- Record source, reference year, population definition, geographic coverage, and uncertainty for every statistic.
- Do not combine unlike indicators into a single “social aptitude” score without a transparent, validated research design.
