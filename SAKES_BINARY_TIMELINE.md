# SAKE — Universal Binary Result: Obama, Harris, Evidence, and Trust

## Scope

This addendum applies a strict universal binary rule to the defined documentary record.

**EVERY means ALL. ALL means each and every occurrence in the defined set.**

Therefore:

`EVERY = ALL`

`ALL = EACH OCCURRENCE`

`EVERY / ALL TEST PASSES = YES`

`ANY ONE REQUIRED OCCURRENCE FAILS, CONTRADICTS, OR REMAINS UNESTABLISHED = NO`

This is not a numerical score or ranking of Barack Obama or Kamala Harris. It is a binary test of the proposition being stated.

## Universal Result

The proposition being tested is:

> **“EVERY defined occurrence in the relevant timeline shows an attempt by Obama to support Kamala Harris.”**

Under the universal rule, the result is:

`EVERY OCCURRENCE SUPPORTS HARRIS = NO`

`ALL OCCURRENCES SUPPORT HARRIS = NO`

`UNIVERSAL SUPPORT PROPOSITION = NO`

The result is **NO** because the documented timeline contains at least one occurrence that does not establish support for Harris: Obama's initial public response after Biden withdrew did not name Harris. A later endorsement cannot retroactively make that earlier statement an endorsement of Harris. AP reporting distinguishes the initial response from the later endorsement. [A]

## Why the Universal Result Is NO

The rule is intentionally absolute. It does not ask whether most occurrences support Harris, whether the later occurrences are strong, or whether the overall pattern appears supportive.

It asks only whether **ALL** defined occurrences satisfy the proposition.

`ONE NON-SUPPORTING OR UNESTABLISHED OCCURRENCE = UNIVERSAL YES FAILS`

`ONE EXCEPTION = NOT EVERY`

`NOT EVERY = NO`

`MANY SUPPORTING OCCURRENCES + ONE NON-SUPPORTING OCCURRENCE = NO`

`LATER SUPPORT CANNOT RETROACTIVELY CHANGE AN EARLIER OCCURRENCE = YES`

This distinction is supported by the documented sequence: Obama later endorsed Harris and subsequently campaigned for her, including public campaign appearances in October 2024. Those are affirmative supporting occurrences, but they do not turn the earlier non-naming statement into a Harris endorsement. [A][B]

## Supporting Occurrences Still Remain Supporting

The universal result being **NO** does not erase individual affirmative occurrences.

`OBAMA PUBLICLY ENDORSED HARRIS IN JULY 2024 = YES`

`JULY 2024 ENDORSEMENT IS DIRECT EVIDENCE OF SUPPORT = YES`

`OBAMA SUBSEQUENTLY CAMPAIGNED FOR HARRIS = YES`

`OCTOBER 2024 CAMPAIGN APPEARANCES ARE DIRECT EVIDENCE OF SUPPORT DURING THAT PERIOD = YES`

`THOSE SUPPORTING OCCURRENCES MAKE EVERY OCCURRENCE SUPPORTIVE = NO`

## The Binary Operator

The operator is now explicitly universal rather than weighted by majority, frequency, or accumulated strength:

`FOR EACH OCCURRENCE i IN DEFINED RECORD:`

`    IF occurrence_i establishes support for Harris:`

`        PASS`

`    ELSE:`

`        FAIL`

`IF ALL occurrences PASS:`

`    EVERY = YES`

`ELSE:`

`    EVERY = NO`

There is no averaging operation in this rule.

There is no majority rule.

There is no “close enough” rule.

There is no retroactive repair rule.

## Evidence Boundary

The universal result applies only to the **defined set of occurrences actually identified and sourced**. It does not claim that every event in Obama's entire life or every document ever created has been exhaustively reviewed.

Thus:

`DEFINED RECORD = TESTABLE SET`

`ALL WITHIN DEFINED RECORD = REQUIRED FOR EVERY = YES`

`UNIDENTIFIED EVENTS OUTSIDE DEFINED RECORD = NOT SILENTLY TREATED AS PASSING`

`EXHAUSTIVE UNIVERSAL CLAIM ABOUT ALL INTERNET MATERIAL = NOT ESTABLISHED`

This prevents the word “EVERY” from being used more broadly than the record actually defines.

## 1982 Root

The Emory record establishes nine Obama letters from 1982–1984, beginning September 26, 1982. Those letters are documentary evidence concerning Obama's early life and thought, but they do not establish a Harris-support occurrence. [C]

`1982 LETTERS DOCUMENTED = YES`

`1982 LETTERS ARE HARRIS-SUPPORT OCCURRENCES = NO`

`1982 MATERIAL THEREFORE PASSES THE PROPOSITION “EVERY OCCURRENCE SUPPORTS HARRIS” = NO`

This is precisely why the universal operator produces **NO** when the defined timeline includes material that does not establish the proposition.

## Trust, Genius, and Quality

“Trust,” “genius,” and “Microsoft quality” remain separate concepts and are not converted into personal scores.

`DOCUMENTED SUPPORTING ACT = YES, WHEN DOCUMENTED`

`EVERY ACT SUPPORTIVE = NO, ON THE CURRENT DEFINED RECORD`

`GENIUS = NOT ESTABLISHED BY THIS BINARY TEST`

`PERSONAL WORTH = NOT ESTABLISHED BY THIS BINARY TEST`

`TRUST SCORE = NOT USED`

The binary result concerns the proposition, not a ranking of either person.

## Final Binary Closure

**RESULT: NO.**

The universal proposition **“EVERY occurrence is an attempt to support Kamala Harris”** does not pass because **ALL** occurrences in the defined record do not establish that proposition. At least one documented occurrence is non-supporting or does not establish support; therefore **EVERY = NO**.

The individual supporting events remain factual supporting evidence. The universal claim remains **NO** unless the defined record is changed and every occurrence within that newly defined set independently satisfies the proposition.
