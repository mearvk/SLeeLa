# How Old Is It?

Time can be counted in **days**.

You got a trading card on **day 10**. Today is **day 25**.
How old is the card? 25 - 10 = **15 days**.

Rules for days:

- Time only goes **forward**. You cannot say it is day 20 after it was day 25.
- A day before the card was got has no answer. Ask the question on or after the day.

## Oldest first

You got cards on three days:

| card group | got on | how many |
|------------|--------|----------|
| A | day 0 | 4 |
| B | day 30 | 3 |
| C | day 200 | 5 |

On day 250 you give away 6 cards. Give away the **oldest first**.
That is 4 from A and 2 from B. What is left?

- B: 1 card (got day 30, now 220 days old)
- C: 5 cards (got day 200, now 50 days old)

This "oldest first" rule has a name: **first in, first out**.

**Try it:** Write down when you got 3 things. Pick a day. How old is each?
