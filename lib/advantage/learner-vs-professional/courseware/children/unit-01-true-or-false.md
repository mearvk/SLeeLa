# Unit 1 (Children): True or False

**You will learn:** a statement is either true or false, and you can check it
by looking. You will also learn to say "I did not look there" when you have not
looked.

**You will need:** the toy shelf picture below, or a real shelf with two boxes.
Then run the [Device Instructor lessons 1 and 2](../../series/README.md) with a
grown-up.

## The toy shelf

The shelf has two boxes and one block.

- The **red box** has the **ball**.
- The **blue box** has the **book**.
- The **block** sits on the shelf, not in a box.

Our **field** is this one shelf. We only talk about what we can see on it.

## Part 1: Look, then say true or false

A statement is **true** if it matches what you see, and **false** if it does not.

Say true or false:

1. The ball is in the red box.
2. The book is in the red box.
3. The block is in the blue box.
4. The block is on the shelf.

## Part 2: AND, OR, NOT

- **AND** is true only when both parts are true.
- **OR** is true when at least one part is true.
- **NOT** flips true to false and false to true.

Say true or false:

5. The ball is in the red box AND the book is in the blue box.
6. The ball is in the red box AND the book is in the red box.
7. The ball is in the red box OR the book is in the red box.
8. NOT (the block is in a box).

## Part 3: If this, then that

A rule says: **if** something is in the red box, **then** it is the ball.

9. The ball is in the red box. Does the rule hold for the ball?
10. A kite is in the red box. Does the rule still hold? (Look at what the rule says.)

## Part 4: "I did not look there"

11. Is there a kite on the shelf? Answer with one of: yes, no, or "not on this
    shelf, I only looked at this one".

12. Someone says "the ball is in the green box". The shelf has no green box.
    What do you say?

## Part 5: Try it with the Device Instructor

With a grown-up, run lesson 1. Find:

- the line where the Instructor says "declare the field of observation first".
  Why do you think it asks for the field first?
- the line for the kite. What does the Instructor say?

## Answer key

1. True. 2. False. 3. False. 4. True.
5. True (both parts are true).
6. False (the second part is false).
7. True (the first part is true).
8. True (the block is not in a box, so "in a box" is false, and NOT flips it).
9. Yes. The rule is about things in the red box, and the ball is the thing there.
10. No. A kite in the red box is not the ball, so the rule would be broken.
11. "Not on this shelf, I only looked at this one." (A "no" for the whole world
    would be a guess.)
12. "There is no green box on this shelf, so I cannot say."
    The Instructor does the same: it refuses a carrier nobody has registered.

Lesson 1 notes: the Instructor needs the field first so it knows where "true"
is checked. For the kite it says "not observed (not in field toy shelf)".

## Check yourself

- I can say true or false by looking.
- I can use AND, OR and NOT.
- I can say "I did not look there" instead of guessing.
