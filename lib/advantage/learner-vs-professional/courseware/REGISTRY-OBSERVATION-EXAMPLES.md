# Registry Observation Examples

Four examples, from plain to applied. In each, the student first fixes the
field, then observes, then states what is known and how. The final step shows
the Purchase Master reaching the same result independently.

## Example 1: The toy shelf (children)

**Field:** the one shelf in the picture. Nothing else.

| noun | carrier | place |
|------|---------|-------|
| ball | red box | shelf, left |
| book | blue box | shelf, left |
| block | shelf itself | shelf, right |

**Observations (each follows the last):**
1. I see two boxes and the shelf. These are the carriers.
2. I open the red box and see a ball. The ball is carried by the red box.
3. I open the blue box and see a book.
4. The block is not in a box. The shelf carries it directly.

**Knowing-knowing:** "I know the ball is in the red box because I opened the
red box and saw it."

**Agreement:** the teacher fills the same table from the same shelf. The tables
match, so the work is accepted. If the teacher wrote the ball in the blue box,
the student re-opens only the blue and red boxes.

## Example 2: The class list (children)

**Field:** the list titled "Class 3" on the wall.

| noun | carrier |
|------|---------|
| Asha, Ben, Chloe | list "Class 3" |

1. The list is the carrier. I count three names.
2. Each name appears once, so each noun has one place.
3. "Dev" is not on the list. The answer is "not in the field", not "absent
   from school".

**Lesson:** a missing entry only tells you about the field you looked at.

## Example 3: A record and its fields (adults)

**Field:** the single record `customer-17`.

| noun | carrier | value |
|------|---------|-------|
| name | record `customer-17` | "A. Rivera" |
| city | record `customer-17` | "Durham" |
| order list | record `customer-17` | 3 entries |
| order 1..3 | the order list | ids 101, 102, 103 |

Here a Noun-Carrier (the order list) is itself a noun carried by another
carrier (the record). The student states the chain: `order 2` is carried by
the order list, which is carried by `customer-17`.

**Placement check:** every noun has exactly one immediate carrier. If `order 2`
appeared in two lists, the placement fails the check and the registry is
corrected before any reasoning continues.

**Agreement:** the Purchase Master lists the same nouns, carriers and chain.
Differences are settled by looking at the record again, not by argument.

## Example 4: Registry to onion (adults)

**Field:** a purchase decision for one item, using only the quotes in hand.

| onion layer | what the student writes |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| dividend | the item and its quoted price |
| review | re-read each quote; confirm each noun and its carrier |
| concerns | what is outside the field (quotes not in hand) |
| maths | total cost from the registered values only |
| additions / subtraction | fees added, discounts removed, each with its source |
| regrouping | group the quotes by supplier carrier |
| iteration | repeat if the Purchase Master's total differs |

The result is accepted when the student's total and the Purchase Master's
total match and every figure traces to a registered noun and its carrier.

## What the student should be able to say

- "This is my field." 
- "These are the nouns and these are their carriers."
- "I know this because I looked at this entry."
- "This is not in my field, so I do not claim it."
- "My result and the Purchase Master's result agree, and here is why."
