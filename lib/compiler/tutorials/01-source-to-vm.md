# Tutorial 01 — SLeeLa Source to VM

Follow a SLeeLa source unit through loading, parsing, declaration discovery, name/symbol resolution, semantic analysis, SLeeLa IR construction, capability/dependency review, VM lowering, SLVM/SLJVM emission, and diagnostics.

The .sleela compiler definitions remain authoritative. Native C/C++ provides implementation services and may not bypass SLeeLa capability, resolver, memory, certificate, security, or VM boundaries.

Inspect LanguageReference.sleela, ProgramMapping.sleela, BinaryFormatReference.sleela, SafetyReference.sleela, and SleelaVMCompilerManager.sleela.
