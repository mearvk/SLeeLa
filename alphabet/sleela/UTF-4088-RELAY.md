## Shared conventions

Spatial traversal is top-left to right, then down, then next Z-layer. Each element uses depth/pressure iteration levels 1–50 unless an explicitly versioned adaptive scheduling mode is selected. These are algorithmic iteration values, not physical forces or electrical signals. Candidate probing priority, nearest-match order, stable catalogue ID, and final alphabetic rank remain separate.

See [the general SLeeLa alphabet specification](SPECIFICATION.md) for general character-set terminology. UTF-4088 is experimental and is not a standard Unicode encoding.
