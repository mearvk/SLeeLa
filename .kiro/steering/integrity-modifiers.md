# Integrity modifiers — 1751 and 1491

Two standing principles distilled from the `stanford-math` archive's dated
modifier documents. They extend (do not replace) the Integrity Goal
([`stanford-math/INTEGRITY-GOAL.md`](../../stanford-math/INTEGRITY-GOAL.md)):
*allow greatness to be recorded — fully and forward — without inflating it, and
credit every reach to its real origin.*

These are the two "modifier schedules" the archive records, kept here as
go-forward settings so the principles apply across the work, not just inside
`stanford-math`.

## 1751 — "unified, not single"

Source: [`stanford-math/1751.md`](../../stanford-math/1751.md). Anchored to the
1751 *Encyclopédie* of Diderot and d'Alembert — many named contributors, one
coherent work.

**The meaning.** The work is **one body built of many faithful parts**. A part
stands on its own, but **no single part carries the standing of the whole**.

Carry it forward like this:

- **Unified.** Many records/modules/files compose into one archive or system;
  the whole is greater than any one part.
- **Not single.** No lone piece claims the authority of the group. None is
  elevated above the rest; none is dropped for convenience.
- The group's wellness is its **coherence**: every entry sourced, every author
  credited, every boundary marked. Unity is *earned by honest parts*, not
  asserted over them.
- It ranks nothing and no one. It divides only **roles** — part from whole,
  record from archive — never people.

## 1491 — "a bond is kept by its symmetry"

Source: [`stanford-math/1491.md`](../../stanford-math/1491.md). Anchored to
quattrocento Italy's reading of friendship and marriage through Cicero,
Aristotle, and Alberti (conjugal *amicizia*).

**The meaning.** A lasting bond needs **both sides in balance** — the high ideal
and the daily affection. **Reach without warmth is cold; warmth without reach is
shallow.** Lose either and the bond tilts.

Carry it forward like this:

- **Keep the symmetry.** Any lasting bond — between people, between a record and
  its sources, between a module and the system — holds both the principled ideal
  *and* the everyday regard that sustains it.
- **Choose in virtue** — "a friend is another self," so the bond is only as
  sound as what it is founded on.
- **Duty is part of love, not its opposite.** Hold obligation and affection
  together rather than splitting them.
- **The durable form is the ideal folded into the ordinary** — Alberti's
  conjugal *amicizia*: the high aim made also an affectionate, working practice.

## How these bind

- Both are **sourced, not invented**; unknowns stay unknown. They record a
  historical understanding faithfully and claim no doctrine beyond what the
  cited record supports (Cicero, Aristotle, Alberti; the 1751 *Encyclopédie*).
- They **extend** the Integrity Goal and the always-on Metadata Class standard
  ([`metadata-class-standard.md`](./metadata-class-standard.md)); they do not
  override them.
- When the two meet: build **one unified whole from many honest parts (1751)**,
  and **keep each bond within it symmetric — ideal balanced with regard (1491)**.
