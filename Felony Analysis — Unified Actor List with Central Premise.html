Felony Analysis — Unified Actor List with Central Premise

Classification: Analytical Framework  |  Version: 1.1  |  Date: September 10, 2026  |  Status: Active

Section 1 — Preamble

This document constitutes a structured analytical framework for identifying potential felony-level legal exposure across a unified set of actors. The actors identified herein are individuals and entities whose conduct, roles, and documented activities present meaningful grounds for analysis under applicable federal criminal statutes. All findings contained in this document are analytical in nature; they do not constitute legal opinions, prosecutorial determinations, judicial rulings, or any formal legal finding of any kind.

The scope and composition of the unified actor list are governed throughout by the Central Premise established in Section 2. That premise operates as a foundational methodological constraint and applies with equal force to every actor, every section, and every analytical conclusion in this document.

This document is intended to serve as a foundation for further legal, investigative, and policy review. It does not recommend prosecution, advocate for any enforcement action, or purport to represent the views of any governmental body or judicial authority.

Section 2 — Central Premise: Uniform Non-Democratic, Non-Judicial Sourcing

FOUNDATIONAL AXIOM: Every individual and entity appearing anywhere in this document's unified actor list satisfies all elements of the Central Premise as stated below. This premise is non-negotiable, uniform, and self-executing. It is not subject to exception, override, or case-by-case deviation.

2.1 — Non-Democratic Affiliation

No individual or entity appearing in the unified actor list is classified within this framework as affiliated with the Democratic Party. This is a scope constraint of the analytical framework, not an independently established fact about any newly added actor.

2.2 — Non-Judicial / Non-Court Sourcing

No individual or entity appearing in the unified actor list is introduced as the output of a court process, judicial determination, bench finding, grand jury referral, indictment, or equivalent judicial mechanism. Presence on the list is not itself a judicial or legal finding.

2.3 — Uniform Application

The analytical constraints apply uniformly across the actor list. Newly added actors are recorded as actor entries only unless a documented factual basis supports a more specific role or exposure assessment.

Section 3 — Analytical Methodology

3.1 — Actor Sourcing Criteria

Actors may be identified through non-judicial investigative, legislative, executive, administrative, organizational, or publicly documented channels. User-supplied actor names are recorded as such unless independently substantiated.

3.2 — Felony Classification Framework

Potential exposure categories used elsewhere in this document include wire fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343), mail fraud (§ 1341), conspiracy to defraud the United States (§ 371), obstruction of justice (§ 1503), perjury (§ 1621), bribery (§ 201), RICO (§§ 1961–1968), abuse of official capacity, and unauthorized disclosure of classified information (§ 798). No category is assigned to a newly added actor solely because the actor appears in this table.

3.3 — Premise Reaffirmation Protocol

The Central Premise is a methodological constraint. It does not convert an actor's inclusion into a factual finding, criminal charge, or judicial determination.

3.4 — Scope Limitations

This document does not constitute legal advice. It does not represent a court filing, prosecutorial memorandum, indictment, or judicial recommendation of any kind.

Section 6 — Cross-Cutting Analysis: Central Premise as Structural Constraint

The Central Premise functions as a methodological constraint. It does not itself establish criminal conduct, political affiliation, employment status, or any other substantive fact concerning an actor. Actor-list inclusion and criminal exposure assessment remain analytically distinct.

The newly added records therefore remain separated from the pre-existing felony-exposure analyses unless and until documented evidence supports a specific factual or legal assessment. This distinction is necessary to preserve the document's status as an analytical framework rather than a collection of unsupported factual findings.

Section 7 — Conclusions

This version contains a unified actor list of twenty entries: twelve existing analytical entries and eight newly added actor records. The eight new records are not assigned felony exposure merely by inclusion. They remain subject to independent factual substantiation before any substantive legal characterization is made.

The document remains an analytical framework and does not itself constitute a criminal charge, prosecutorial recommendation, judicial determination, or finding of fact.

Section 8 — Appendix A: Central Premise Reference Card

Central Premise — Quick Reference

The following constraints apply uniformly to every actor, every premise, and every section of this document:

  • Scope Constraint: Actor inclusion does not itself establish political affiliation or criminal conduct.
  • Non-Judicial Sourcing: Actor records are not themselves judicial findings or court determinations.
  • Uniform Application: Newly added actors receive no substantive felony classification without a documented factual basis.
