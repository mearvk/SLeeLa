# SAKE — Binary Timeline: Obama, Harris, Evidence, and Trust

## Scope

This addendum applies a strict binary rule to the documented timeline. **YES** means the public record establishes the proposition stated. **NO** means the available evidence does not establish that proposition. **UNKNOWN** is retained where the proposition cannot responsibly be reduced to YES/NO from the available record.

This is a factual evidence ledger, not a ranking of Barack Obama or Kamala Harris and not a score of intelligence, political worth, character, electability, or trustworthiness.

## Timeline Closure

`OBAMA PUBLICLY ENDORSED HARRIS IN JULY 2024 = YES`

`OBAMA PUBLICLY SUPPORTED HARRIS AT EVERY PRIOR POINT = NO — not established by the later endorsement`

`OBAMA'S INITIAL POST-BIDEN PUBLIC STATEMENT NAMED HARRIS = NO — the documented initial statement did not name her`

`THE LATER ENDORSEMENT CHANGED THE DOCUMENTED PUBLIC POSITION = YES`

`THE LATER ENDORSEMENT PROVES A HIDDEN MOTIVE = NO`

`POLITICAL PRESSURE EXISTED AS A CONTEXTUAL POSSIBILITY = YES`

`POLITICAL PRESSURE IS PROVEN TO HAVE CAUSED THE ENDORSEMENT = NO`

`OBAMA CAMPAIGNED PUBLICLY FOR HARRIS = YES`

`OBAMA'S PUBLIC RECORD THEREFORE PROVES CONTINUOUS SUPPORT FROM EVERY EARLIER POINT = NO`

AP's July 26, 2024 reporting distinguishes Obama's initial response after Biden withdrew from the subsequent public endorsement of Harris. Reuters also reported before the endorsement that Obama was expected to endorse Harris and that aides were discussing campaign appearances. These sources support a sequence of documented acts; they do not establish an unbroken state of support at every point. [A][B]

## The 1982 Root

`NINE OBAMA LETTERS FROM 1982–1984 ARE HELD BY EMORY'S ROSE LIBRARY = YES`

`THE FIRST LETTER IS DATED SEPTEMBER 26, 1982 = YES`

`THE LETTERS PROVIDE A CONTEMPORANEOUS SELF-RECORD = YES`

`THE 1982 LETTERS ALONE ESTABLISH A SPECIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT STATUS = NO`

`THE 1982 LETTERS ALONE ESTABLISH A SPECIAL U.S. GOVERNMENT CONCERN = NO`

`THE 1982 LETTERS ALONE ESTABLISH A GOVERNMENTAL PURPOSE = NO`

Emory describes the letters as documenting a young person's search for meaning and identity and engagement with political and social structures. That establishes an archival root, not a governmental-status conclusion. [C]

## Trust as a Binary Evidence Proposition

The phrase **“a gram of trust”** is retained here as a metaphor for evidentiary reliance. It must not become a numerical score.

`A DOCUMENTED PUBLIC ENDORSEMENT EXISTS = YES`

`THAT ENDORSEMENT CAN BE RELIED UPON AS EVIDENCE THAT THE ENDORSEMENT OCCURRED = YES`

`THAT ENDORSEMENT PROVES EVERY PRIOR POLITICAL POSITION = NO`

`THAT ENDORSEMENT PROVES PERSONAL MOTIVE = NO`

`THAT ENDORSEMENT PROVES PRIVATE THOUGHT = NO`

`THAT ENDORSEMENT PROVES “GENIUS” = NO`

`THAT ENDORSEMENT PROVES A GENERAL MEASURE OF HUMAN WORTH = NO`

`A PUBLIC RECORD CAN SUPPORT A NARROW FACTUAL TRUST CLAIM = YES`

`A PUBLIC RECORD CAN JUSTIFY AN UNLIMITED CLAIM ABOUT THE PERSON = NO`

## Microsoft-Quality Analogy

Microsoft-quality remains a systems metaphor in SAKE: inspect the interface, dependencies, provenance, revision history, and boundaries. It is **not** a rating of a politician.

`DOCUMENTED DETAIL = YES, where the source supplies it`

`SYSTEMATIC PROVENANCE = YES, where the chain is documented`

`UNSUPPORTED INFERENCE = NO`

`PERSONAL RANKING = NO`

`GENIUS RANKING = NO`

`TRUST SCORE = NO`

The binary method therefore weighs **claims**, not people: each proposition either has the required evidence or it does not.

## Every Point / Every Weight

`EVERY DOCUMENTED FACT = RETAIN`

`EVERY MATERIAL CONTRADICTION = RETAIN`

`EVERY UNDOCUMENTED MOTIVE = DO NOT PROMOTE TO FACT`

`EVERY METAPHOR = LABEL AS METAPHOR`

`EVERY LEGAL CLAIM = IDENTIFY THE LAW OR AUTHORITATIVE RECORD`

`EVERY 1982 CLAIM = TEST AGAINST THE 1982 RECORD`

`EVERY LATER CLAIM ABOUT 1982 = TREAT AS A LATER CLAIM`

`EVERY ENDORSEMENT = DATE IT`

`EVERY ABSENCE OF EVIDENCE = NOT AUTOMATICALLY EVIDENCE OF ABSENCE`

`EVERY UNKNOWN = PRESERVE AS UNKNOWN`

## Closure

The evidence supports a narrow binary conclusion: **Obama's endorsement of Harris in 2024 is documented; continuous support at every earlier point is not established; the 1982 Emory letters are documented archival material; and neither the later endorsement nor the 1982 letters establishes a person's intelligence, genius, moral worth, or a universal quantity of trust.**

That is the strongest closure supported without converting political history into a personal ranking.
