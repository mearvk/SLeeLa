# Unit 4 (Children): Steps in Order

**You will learn:** that some jobs must be done in order, and how to repeat a
step. **Use with:** [lesson 2, the student input gate](../../series/README.md).

## Recipes need order

To make toast: 1. put bread in the toaster, 2. push the lever down, 3. wait,
4. take the toast out.

1. What goes wrong if you take the toast out first?
2. Put these in order: spread butter, wait for toast, put bread in, lever down.

## Counting steps

The Instructor numbers each step. It only accepts the **next** number.

Last accepted step is 1.

3. Can you send step 2? Step 3? Step 1 again?
4. You sent step 2 and it was accepted. What is the next step?

In lesson 2, the Instructor refuses step 3 when the last accepted step is 1.
It says: "this step does not follow the last accepted step". Run it with a
grown-up and find that line.

## Repeating a step

"Do it 3 times" means: do it, do it, do it. Then stop.

5. Put 3 blocks on the shelf, one at a time. Count out loud.
6. A friend says "keep adding blocks". What is missing from this rule?

## Good steps have proof

Each step also says **what you looked at**. The Instructor refuses a step that
points at something that is not on its list.

7. Step 2 says "the book is in the green box". There is no green box. What does
   the Instructor say?

## Answer key

1. There is no toast yet; the order matters.
2. Put bread in, lever down, wait for toast, spread butter.
3. Step 2 only. Step 3 skips a step and step 1 is already done.
4. Step 3.
5. One, two, three. Then stop.
6. A place to stop. Say how many, or say when to stop.
7. "refused: evidence is not an entry in the registry".
