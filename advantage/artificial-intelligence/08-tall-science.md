# Lesson 08 — Tall Science: the United States as an Item (advanced)

> Series: [index](README.md) · prev: [07](07-the-moral-evaluator.md)

`SLTallScience` holds the United States as a cataloged **Item**: a name, lists of
subject nouns, names, scientific individuals and moral individuals, and boundary
and respect flags. It is a reference record that a trade is judged against.
Nothing is sold.

## Respectively

Nouns and names pair by position. If you register a noun without its name, the
pairing point fails; register the name and it passes. Try it in section 2 of the demo.

## Individuals

The lists hold role labels (`scientist (role)`) or public-record names the caller
supplies. Do not store private data about people here.

## Exercises

1. Remove the moral individuals. Which point fails and does the trade still pass?
2. Why does a missing catalog make the whole dimension fail rather than be skipped?
3. Design a point that checks a boundary other than the US. What generalizes?
