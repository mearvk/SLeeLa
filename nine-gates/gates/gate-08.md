## Motif

A compass rose: an outer ring with marked cardinal points and a four-pointed needle pivoting on a
small hub at the center.

## Meaning

The eighth gate is **direction regained**. With the ember kept alive, the task is to choose a true
course again rather than drift — to reorient after the disorder of the middle gates and point
deliberately toward the end. It stands for renewed purpose: not the first naive direction from the
threshold, but a chosen bearing informed by everything learned since.
