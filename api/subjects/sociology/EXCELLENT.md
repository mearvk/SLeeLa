# Sociology Subject Library — Excellent Standard

The Subject Library is considered Excellent when definitions are explicit, quantities have declared dimensions or units, observed and derived values remain distinct, formulas have bounded numerical behavior, assumptions and study-design limits are documented, XML can describe the model and procedure without becoming executable code, native C/C++ source is independently testable, SLeeLa examples demonstrate the vocabulary, Nordshrift can carry the subject through its canonical semantic chain, and provenance plus validation remain explicit.

Excellent is a repository engineering target, not a claim that every sociological method is complete or scientifically settled.
